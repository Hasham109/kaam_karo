## 3. Verbatim Acceptance Criteria Verification Detail (IMPL §6 T3.3)

1. **"Confidence < 0.6 fallback to clarify (test)"**
   - *Verified:* `IntakeService.interpret` and `FakeIntakeAiClient` degrade confidence to 0.40 (< 0.60 threshold) when customer input is ambiguous or underspecified, returning exactly 1-2 clarifying questions. Verified in unit tests: *"should fall back to clarifying questions when confidence is below 0.60"*.
2. **"catalog-invalid output rejected (test)"**
   - *Verified:* When AI suggests a service ID or slug that does not exist or is inactive in the catalog, `IntakeService.interpret` sets `serviceId = null`, forces confidence to 0.35, and prompts the customer to re-select from the verified catalog. Verified in unit tests: *"should reject catalog-invalid output and degrade confidence"*.
3. **"no raw prompt with PII in logs (test)"**
   - *Verified:* `scrubPii()` utility scrubs Pakistani mobile numbers (`03xx-xxxxxxx`, `+923xxxxxxxxx`), CNIC formats (`XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X`), and email addresses. DB integration test and unit test verify that the stored `ai_interaction.promptScrubbed` contains `[PHONE_REDACTED]`, `[CNIC_REDACTED]`, and `[EMAIL_REDACTED]`, and zero raw PII.
4. **"Golden tests on the Blueprint examples"**
   - *Verified:* Unit test suite executes the exact Blueprint examples:
     - BP §56 / §66 AC repair Urdu audio transcript: *"میرا اے سی آن نہیں ہو رہا اور ٹھنڈی ہوا نہیں دے رہا"* -> Maps to `ac-repair`, `cooling_failure`, confidence 0.92.
     - BP §56 Electrician short circuit English prompt: *"Short circuit in switchboard, sparks coming out"* -> Maps to `electrician-general`, `urgent` urgency, confidence 0.90.
     - BP §66 Geyser repair with location & time hint: *"Water heater leaking in DHA Phase 4, need someone today evening"* -> Maps to `geyser-service`, `DHA Phase 4` location hint, `TODAY_EVENING` time hint, confidence 0.88.
