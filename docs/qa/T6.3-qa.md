## 1. Scope & Verification Matrix

| Item | Requirement IDs | Design Decisions | Status | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Refunds as Reversal Rows** | `PAY-004`, `PAY-006`, `SUP-004` | `ARCH §6.8`, `BP §25, §26` | **PASS** | Original payment row is never updated in place. Refunds are append-only rows (`ISSUED` → `SUCCEEDED` / `FAILED`) linked by `paymentId` and `bookingId`. |
| **Refund Cap & Partial Refunds** | `PAY-006`, `OQ-058` | `BP §26` | **PASS** | Total refund amount capped at paid digital amount minus prior non-failed refunds. Partial refunds succeed up to cap; exceeding throws `409 REFUND_EXCEEDS_REFUNDABLE`. |
| **Cash Payment Invariant** | `PAY-006`, `D-016` | `D-030` | **PASS** | Cash payments cannot be refunded via gateway flow (`409 NOTHING_REFUNDABLE`). |
| **Refund Ledger Reversal** | `PAY-006`, `LEDGER-005` | `ARCH §6.8` | **PASS** | On gateway confirmation of refund, proportional `REVERSAL` debit is written to provider's ledger reversing provider's share of `JOB_CREDIT`. |
| **Mandatory Audit Reason** | `ROLE-003`, `ADMIN-ROLE-004` | `BP §26` | **PASS** | Missing/empty reason on refunds, settlements, and corrections rejected with `422 REASON_REQUIRED`. |
| **Positive Balance Settlement** | `PAY-007`, `LEDGER-007` | `ARCH §6.8`, `BP §13 P15` | **PASS** | Only positive provider balances can be settled. Non-positive balance throws `409 BALANCE_NOT_POSITIVE`; exceeding balance throws `409 AMOUNT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`. |
| **Settlement Modes** | `PAY-007`, `D-008` | `EXT-001` | **PASS** | `MANUAL` requires reference and marks `PAID` immediately. `PAYOUT_PROVIDER` dispatches to `PayoutProvider` abstraction. |
| **Failed Payout Reversal** | `PAY-007`, `LEDGER-005` | `ARCH §6.8` | **PASS** | Failed payout marks settlement `FAILED` and restores funds via `REVERSAL` credit row. |
| **Ledger Corrections** | `PAY-004`, `LEDGER-005` | `BP §26` | **PASS** | Financial correction creates a `REVERSAL` row in opposite direction (CREDIT ↔ DEBIT) capped at unreversed portion. Original row unchanged. |
| **Reconciliation Job** | `PAY-005` | `A09 Exceptions` | **PASS** | Detects and reports `MISSING_AT_GATEWAY`, `STATUS_MISMATCH`, `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, `STUCK_PAYMENT`, `STUCK_REFUND`, and `DIGITAL_UNCONFIRMED`. |
| **Idempotency** | `REL-003`, `REL-008` | `BP §31.2` | **PASS** | Replayed refund and settlement requests with identical `idempotencyKey` return existing entities without duplicate rows. |
