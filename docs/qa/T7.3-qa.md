| **Rating & Structured Tags** | `REV-001`, `RATING-ENF-001` | `ARCH §4 M12`, `BP §12 C21` | **PASS** | 1–5 star rating enforced. | **Optional Written Review Text** | `REV-002` | `BP §12 C21`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Review text is optional; reviews with rating only are accepted. Max text length enforced from config `reviews.max_text_length` (default 1000 chars); exceeding returns 422 `TEXT_TOO_LONG`. |
| **Provider Single Response** | `REV-003` | `BP §13 P17`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Assigned provider can respond once under controlled rules. Responding to another provider's booking returns 403 `FORBIDDEN`; duplicate response returns 409 `ALREADY_RESPONDED`. |
| **Flag / Report Flow** | `REV-004` | `BP §12 C21`, `BP §13 P17`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Customer or provider can flag review content with mandatory reason (min 5 chars). Review state transitions to `FLAGGED` and enqueues into Risk & Trust moderation. |
| **Moderation Queue & Actions** | `REV-005`, `ROLE-003` | `BP §14 A11`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Moderation decisions (`HIDE` or `KEEP`) require mandatory audit reason (min 5 chars) logged via `AuditWriter`. `HIDE` sets `Review.moderationStatus` to `HIDDEN`; `KEEP` dismisses flag and maintains `VISIBLE`. |
| **Canonical Public Rating Calculation** | `REV-006`, `RATING-ENF-002` | `rating.util.ts`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Canonical `computePublicRating` filters strictly `ReviewModerationStatus.VISIBLE`. Hidden or non-visible reviews excluded. Arithmetic mean rounded to 1 decimal place. |
| **Review Integrity Invariant** | `REV-007` | `BP §19` | **PASS** | Providers cannot buy or manufacture ratings. Only the booking's customer after `Completed` can create a review; providers cannot edit or delete ratings. |
| **Review Eligibility Window** | `CFG-RULE-009` | `BP §12 C21`, `BP §19` | **PASS** | Dynamic window checked against config `reviews.eligibility_window_days` (default 30 days). Expired reviews rejected with 409 `REVIEW_WINDOW_CLOSED`. If null, any completed booking is eligible. |
| **Role-Gated Admin Moderation** | `OPS-ADMIN-011`, `ADMIN-ROLE-006` | `BP §8.4`, `admin.md §1.6` | **PASS** | A11 Reviews & Moderation Queue is strictly restricted to Risk & Trust (`RISK_TRUST`) sub-role. Non-RT staff receive `403 Forbidden` (`FORBIDDEN_SUB_ROLE`). |
| **Customer App C21 Screen** | `UX-CUST-005` | `docs/screens/customer.md C21` | **PASS** | Customer Rate & Review screen implemented with 5 canonical states (`L`, `E`, `S`, `X`, `P`), 1–5 star interactive selector, structured tags chips, optional text input, RTL support, and ≥ 48 dp touch targets. |
