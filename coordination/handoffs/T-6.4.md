## 1. Summary of Completed Work

### 1. Customer App UI Screens
1. **C16 Payment Screen (`apps/customer/src/screens/C16PaymentScreen.tsx`):**
   - **Bill Line Items:** Itemized labour lines, materials/parts lines, and explicit launch tax row (Rs. 0 per D-029).
   - **Platform Fee Concealment:** Strictly adheres to D-025 (no platform fee row displayed).
   - **Post-Completion Timing Gating (D-003, PAY-TIMING-001):** Pay button is disabled and explicit guidance message is shown if booking status is not `COMPLETED`.
   - **Cash Payment Policy Invariant (D-030):** Zero cash selection button for customers. Read-only informational note explaining that cash payments are settled directly with and recorded by the partner.
   - **Digital Hosted Gateway Trigger:** Hosted checkout redirect trigger with confirmation dialog.
   - **Payment Status Chips:** Displays canonical statuses (Not paid / Processing / Paid / Failed).
   - **Offline / Network Loss Resiliency (BP §31.1):** Automatically transitions to Pending state and disables payment trigger on connectivity drops.
   - **5 Canonical States:** Loading, Empty, Success, Error, Pending.
   - **Typography & Layout:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu (2.5x line-height), RTL mirroring, min 48×48 dp tap targets, viewport ≤ 360 dp support.

2. **C24 Payment Receipts Screen (`apps/customer/src/screens/C24PaymentReceiptsScreen.tsx`):**
   - **Transaction & Receipt History:** Lists historical payments and receipts with receipt modal breakdown.
   - **Reversal Row Linked Display (BP §26, PAY-006):** Displays associated refund/reversal entries linked to the original payment.
   - **Share Receipt (`ACTION_SEND`, SHARE-001):** Share receipt summary via system share payload.
   - **5 Canonical States:** Loading, Empty, Success, Error, Pending.
