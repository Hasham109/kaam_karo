## 1. Summary of Completed Work

1. **In-App Chat Bound to Booking Participants (`CHAT-001`, `BP §20, §25`):**
   - Created `services/api/src/modules/chat/chat.service.ts` and `chat.controller.ts`.
   - `validateBookingParticipant` dynamically asserts that callers belong to the booking (customer or assigned provider) or have `UserRoleType.ADMIN`.
   - Unauthenticated or non-participant callers receive `403 Forbidden` (`NOT_BOOKING_PARTICIPANT`).
   - Conversations bound to `bookingId`.
   - Messaging sends text and attachments, persisting messages and emitting domain event `message.new` to the transactional Outbox for notification fan-out (`NOTIF-007`).

2. **Masked Telephony (`CallRelay`) Interface & Fake (`CHAT-002`, `EXT-001`, `D-009`, `BP §20, §74`):**
   - Defined `CallRelayService` abstraction and implemented `FakeCallRelayService`.
   - Generates virtual relay DID numbers (`+92421115226`, formatted `+92 42 111-KAAM (5226)`) with 30-minute expiry sessions.
   - Calling is restricted to active matched jobs (`ACCEPTED`, `SCHEDULED`, `PROVIDER_EN_ROUTE`, `ARRIVED`, `IN_PROGRESS`).
   - Supports test-only fault injection modes: `ok`, `slow`, `timeout`, `error` (`docs/qa/test-strategy.md` §3).
   - Invariant verified: Zero raw personal phone numbers in any payload or UI.

3. **Attachment Validation & Size Limits (`CHAT-003`, `D-011`):**
   - Strictly enforces allowed MIME types for chat: `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `image/webp`, and `application/pdf`. Disallowed types rejected with `400 Bad Request`.
   - Dynamically checks file size against `media.chat.max_bytes` (default 5MB). Oversized files rejected with `400 Bad Request`.

4. **Intent-Only Telephony & WhatsApp Hand-off (`CHAT-004`, `M-07`, `BP §74, §82`):**
   - Dialer launched exclusively via standard `ACTION_DIAL` (`tel:`) intent with the virtual relay number.
   - WhatsApp launched exclusively via `ACTION_SEND` intent with share payload text.
   - App never requests or reads call logs, contacts, or WhatsApp application data.

5. **External Share Payloads (`SHARE-001`, `SHARE-002`, `SHARE-003`, `BP §20, §52.2, §82`):**
   - `buildBookingSharePayload`: Assembles customer share payload with service name, broad area (e.g. "Gulberg III, Lahore"), booking reference, and deep link. Strictly omits customer personal phone numbers and exact street addresses.
   - `buildProviderProfileSharePayload`: Builds public profile share payload for providers.
   - `SHARE-003`: Invariant verified that external share links contain no commercial action execution tokens or endpoints.

6. **Free-Text Phone Number Masking (`BP §20`, `admin.md §1.6`):**
   - Implemented `maskPhoneNumbersInText` masking Pakistani mobile and landline patterns (`+92 ••• •••••••`) in free-text message bodies for admin audit views and notification previews.

7. **Customer App C18 Screen (`apps/customer/src/screens/C18ChatContactScreen.tsx`):**
   - Implemented C18 Chat / Contact screen supporting all 5 canonical states (`L`, `E`, `S`, `X`, `P`).
   - Message bubble history with timestamps and delivery status.
   - Input bar with send, attachment trigger, masked call button, safety actions (report/block), and share request button.
   - 100% bilingual in English and Urdu with Noto Nastaliq font, RTL mirroring, and ≥ 48x48 dp touch targets.
   - Wired into `CustomerRouter` in `App.tsx` and linked from tracking screen C17.

8. **OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract:**
   - Published `contracts/chat.yaml` documenting all chat, call-relay, and share endpoints with requirement IDs (`CHAT-001..003`, `SHARE-001..003`).
