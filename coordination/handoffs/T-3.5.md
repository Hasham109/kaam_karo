## What I Built

### 1. OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract (`contracts/quotes.yaml`)
- Published contract for Quotes & Fare under OpenAPI 3.1.0 (semver 0.1.0).
- Registered in `contracts/README.md`.
- Endpoints defined:
  - `POST /v1/quotes`: Submit quote or commercial offer with structured fare lines and mandatory `crew_size >= 1` (`QUOTE-001..005`, `D-004`).
  - `GET /v1/quotes/{id}`: Retrieve quote details, duration, inclusions, exclusions, and fare lines (`QUOTE-005`, `PRICE-006`).
  - `GET /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/quotes`: Multi-supplier quotes comparison side-by-side (`QUOTE-009`, `BP §53`).
  - `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/accept`: Customer accepts quote. Validates validity end date (`QUOTE-004`), copies lines and crew size to booking, sets booking status to `ACCEPTED`, and auto-declines competing offers (`QUOTE-006`, `QUOTE-007`, `QUOTE-009`).
  - `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/decline`: Customer declines quote with reason (`QUOTE-006`).
  - `POST /v1/quotes/{id}/clarification`: Customer asks for clarification without declining (`QUOTE-006`).
  - `POST /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/fare-lines`: Proposes extra charge during work in `PROPOSED` state; automatically moves booking to `AWAITING_APPROVAL` (`FARE-001..013`, `BOOK-STATUS-010`).
  - `GET /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/fare-lines`: Lists all fare lines for a booking.
  - `POST /v1/fare-lines/{id}/approve`: Customer approves proposed extra charge (`FARE-012`). Returns booking to `IN_PROGRESS` when all pending lines are resolved.
  - `POST /v1/fare-lines/{id}/reject`: Customer rejects proposed extra charge (`FARE-012`).
  - `POST /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/crew-size-change`: Provider proposes post-acceptance crew size change; moves booking to `AWAITING_APPROVAL` with Job Event (`ARCH §5.6`, `QUOTE-008`).
  - `POST /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/crew-size-change/decide`: Customer approves or rejects crew size change.
  - `GET /v1/bookings/{bookingId}/payable-summary`: Computes payable total from `APPROVED`/`FINAL` lines only. Unapproved lines are strictly excluded (`FARE-012`, `PRICE-007`). Calculates commissionable labour base (`COMM-003`, `D-015`).
- Validated by `@kaamkaro/contracts` schema tests (37/37 tests pass).

### 2. Quotes & Fare Engine (`services/api/src/modules/quotes/quotes.service.ts`)
- **Verbatim Acceptance Criterion 1 ("Offer without crew size rejected")**:
  - Enforces `crewSize` as a required integer >= 1 on every quote/offer (D-004, `PROV-CREW-001`). Rejects missing, zero, negative, or fractional values with `BadRequestException` (`CREW_SIZE_REQUIRED`). Enforced in DB by `quote_crew_size_positive`.
- **Verbatim Acceptance Criterion 2 ("Unapproved line never payable")**:
  - In `getPayableSummary`: Only lines in `APPROVED` or `FINAL` state are summed into `totalPayableMinor`. Any line in `PROPOSED` or `REJECTED` state is strictly excluded (`FARE-012`, `PRICE-007`).
- **Verbatim Acceptance Criterion 3 ("Crew-size change after acceptance creates an approval request")**:
  - Post-acceptance crew size change (`proposeCrewSizeChange`) transitions booking to `AWAITING_APPROVAL` (`BOOK-STATUS-010`), logs `CREW_SIZE_CHANGE_PROPOSED` Job Event, and awaits customer decision via `decideCrewSizeChange` (`ARCH §5.6`, `QUOTE-008`).
- **Fare Line Type Guards (BP §58.1, FARE-001..013)**:
  - `TOOLS`: Rejects tools lines without a description/reason (`FARE-004`).
  - `PARTS`: Rejects parts lines without `materialsClass` (`PRICE-005`, `FARE-006`).
  - `PLATFORM_FEE`: Customer platform fee disabled at launch (`D-025`, `FARE-009`); attempts are rejected.
  - `EMERGENCY`: Emergency exception flag `fare.emergency_exception_enabled` defaults to false / fails closed (`FARE-013`, `K-10`).
  - `LABOR`: The only commissionable line type (`D-015`, `COMM-003`). No pricing code reads `crew_size` (`D-018`).
- **Quote Expiry & Competitor Deletion (QUOTE-004, QUOTE-009)**:
  - Expired quotes cannot be accepted (`QUOTE_EXPIRED`).
  - Accepting a quote automatically marks all other open quotes for that booking as `DECLINED`.
