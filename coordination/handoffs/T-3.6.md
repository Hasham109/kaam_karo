## What I Built

### 1. OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract (`contracts/availability.yaml`)
- Published contract for Availability & Trust Service under OpenAPI 3.1.0 (semver 0.1.0).
- Registered in `contracts/README.md`.
- Endpoints defined:
  - `PUT /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/schedule`: Atomically replaces weekly schedule and break intervals (`AVAIL-001`, `AVAIL-002`, `AVAIL-004`).
  - `GET /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/schedule`: Retrieves provider's active schedule rules.
  - `POST /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/overrides`: Creates or updates a calendar date override (`AVAIL-003`).
  - `PUT /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/live-status`: Updates provider's operational status (`ONLINE_AVAILABLE`, `ONLINE_BUSY`, `OFFLINE_MANUAL`, `OFFLINE_OUT_OF_SCHEDULE`) (`AVAIL-007`).
  - `PUT /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/service-areas`: Replaces service areas (radius or zone mode) with Pakistan geographic boundary validation (`AVAIL-005`).
  - `GET /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/service-areas`: Retrieves provider's configured service areas.
  - `POST /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/check-slot`: Evaluates slot availability considering working hours, breaks, overrides, capacity, and standing (`MATCH-ELIG-075..079`).
  - `GET /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/standing`: Retrieves provider trust standing (`TRUST-VER-001`).
  - `POST /v1/availability/providers/{providerProfileId}/verification`: Submits verification documents for review (`TRUST-VER-002`).
  - `POST /v1/availability/verification-records/{id}/decision`: Staff-audited approval or rejection elevating verification level (`TRUST-VER-003`).
- Validated by `@kaamkaro/contracts` schema tests (33/33 tests pass).

### 2. Availability & Service Areas Engine (`availability.service.ts`)
- **Weekly Schedule & Breaks (`AVAIL-001`, `AVAIL-002`, `AVAIL-004`):**
  - Validates `startTime < endTime` on all intervals (`HH:mm` format).
  - Validates that breaks fall completely within the day's working hours and do not overlap with one another.
  - Executes atomic bulk replacement inside a database transaction.
- **Date Overrides (`AVAIL-003`):**
  - Supports `OFF` (day off/leave) and `CUSTOM_HOURS`. Overrides take precedence over regular weekly schedules.
- **Geographic Service Areas (`AVAIL-005`):**
  - Radius mode enforces coordinates strictly within Pakistan territory via `isInsidePakistan` (`LOC-PAK-001`) with positive `radius_meters`.
  - Zone mode links to registered `service_zone_id`.
- **Capacity Limits & Live Status (`AVAIL-006`, `AVAIL-007`):**
  - Enforces `max_active_jobs >= 1`.
  - Live status supports 4 operational states.
- **Slot Evaluation Logic (`MATCH-ELIG-075..079`):**
  - Evaluates requested datetime slot against calendar overrides, weekly hours, recurring breaks, current active job capacity, and trust standing.

### 3. Provider Trust & Verification Service (`trust.service.ts`)
- **Initial Standing (`TRUST-VER-001`):** Defaults newly registered providers to `BASIC` verification level and `GOOD_STANDING`.
- **Document Submission (`TRUST-VER-002`):** Records uploaded document media IDs with `PENDING_REVIEW` status.
- **Staff Decisions & Auditing (`TRUST-VER-003`, `ABUSE-010`):** Requires non-empty audit `decisionReason`. Elevates provider to `VERIFIED` or `BUSINESS_VERIFIED` atomically upon approval.
- **Matching Eligibility (`TRUST-VER-004`, `MATCH-ELIG-077`):** Rejects suspended/banned providers and checks level requirements.

### 4. Controller & NestJS Module Integration (`availability.controller.ts`, `availability.module.ts`, `app.module.ts`)
- Registered `AvailabilityModule` in NestJS `AppModule`.
- Exposes authenticated and public endpoints with proper guards.

- Real-time location tracking for en-route providers is handled in downstream module M6 (Tracking); T3.6 provides the static service area coverage and calendar scheduling foundation.
