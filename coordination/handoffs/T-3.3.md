## What I Built

### 1. OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract (`contracts/intake.yaml`)
- Published contract for AI Intake & Request Management under OpenAPI 3.1.0 (semver 0.1.0).
- Registered in `contracts/README.md`.
- Endpoints defined:
  - `POST /v1/intake/interpret`: Interprets natural language prompt/audio transcript into structured closed schema (`REQ-AI-001..004, 006..008, 010`).
  - `POST /v1/intake/requests`: Creates customer request in `COMPOSING` or `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` draft state (`REQ-AI-005, 012`).
  - `GET /v1/intake/requests/{id}`: Retrieves customer request details and snapshot.
  - `POST /v1/intake/requests/{id}/confirm`: Customer confirms request, transitioning to `SUBMITTED` with locked snapshot (`REQ-AI-005, 007, 010, 011`).
  - `GET /v1/intake/requests/{id}/ai-interactions`: Retrieves scrubbed AI interaction history (`REQ-AI-008`).
- Validated by `@kaamkaro/contracts` schema tests (39/39 tests pass).

### 2. Intake Service & State Machine (`services/api/src/modules/intake/intake.service.ts`)
- **Request Lifecycle (`REQ-AI-005`):**
  - Manages transitions: `COMPOSING` -> `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` -> `SUBMITTED`.
  - Rejects confirmation on already submitted or cancelled requests.
  - Generates immutable `request_snapshot` record containing final service, location coordinates, scheduled time window, and emergency status upon submission (`REQ-AI-012`).
- **Catalog Validation (`REQ-AI-007`):**
  - Cross-references suggested service IDs/slugs against active DB catalog via `CatalogService`.
  - Rejects unknown/unverified services, drops confidence to 0.35, and prompts customer for catalog re-selection.
- **Emergency Safeguard (`REQ-AI-011`):**
  - Validates `service.emergencyAllowed = true` before allowing emergency confirmation.
- **Direct Non-AI Fallback (`REQ-AI-009`):**
  - Allows customers to bypass AI parsing and directly select catalog items, addresses, and time slots.

### 3. Fake AI Client & Golden Test Parser (`services/api/src/modules/intake/intake-ai.client.ts`)
- Implements `IntakeAiClient` interface with deterministic mock for Phase 3 testing.
- **Blueprint Golden Examples:**
  - AC cooling failure (Urdu audio transcript / text): Maps to `ac-repair` with confidence 0.92.
  - Electrical short circuit (English prompt): Maps to `electrician-general` with `urgent` priority and confidence 0.90.
  - Geyser leak (English/Roman Urdu): Maps to `geyser-service`, `DHA Phase 4` location hint, `TODAY_EVENING` window, confidence 0.88.
- **Confidence & Ambiguity Fallback (`REQ-AI-003`, `REQ-AI-006`):**
  - Degrades confidence to 0.40 (< 0.60 threshold) when input is ambiguous, returning at most 2 targeted clarifying questions in Urdu or English.
- **Anti-Hallucination (`REQ-AI-010`):**
  - Strictly closed schema: no pricing, provider assignment, arrival ETA, or phone contact fields.

### 4. Privacy & Content Moderation (`services/api/src/modules/intake/intake.types.ts`)
- **PII Scrubbing (`REQ-AI-008`, `SEC-PRIV-001`, `BP §66`):**
  - Automatically scrubs Pakistani mobile phone numbers (`03xx-xxxxxxx`, `+923xxxxxxxxx`), CNICs (`XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X`), and emails before saving to `ai_interaction.promptScrubbed`.
- **Content Moderation (`BP §67`):**
  - Filters out abusive terms or out-of-scope commercial requests with `safetyState = BLOCKED` and safe canned responses.
