Delivered partner job execution, extras proposal, completion evidence, and billing screens covering **P12, P13, and P14** (`BP §13 P12..P14, BP §57.4, BP §81, BP §15, BP §58.1..2; LOC-GEO-007, UX-PROV-004, UX-PROV-005; BOOK-STATUS-006..010, FARE-003..012, PRICE-005, PRICE-007; D-003, D-006, D-016, D-021, D-030`):

1. **Active Job Location Sharing Indicator (`BP §57.4`, `BP §81`, `LOC-GEO-007`):**
   - Verbatim acceptance criteria met: *"Location only shared during active job, indicator visible"*.
   - Location indicator strip rendered **strictly and exclusively** during `Provider En Route` status (`executionStatus === 'EN_ROUTE'`).
   - Automatically and irreversibly terminated upon moving to `Arrived` or any non-en-route status.
   - Provider manual stop toggle available while en route without cancelling the booking.
   - Zero background location tracking.

2. **One-Tap Navigation Hand-off (`BP §81`, `UX-PROV-004`, `G2`, `G3`):**
   - Opens external navigation app (Google Maps intent) with customer confirmed address snapshot.
   - Zero foreground GPS permissions requested or required at launch.

3. **P12 Job Execution Screen (`BP §13 P12`, `BP §15`, `BOOK-STATUS-006..010`):**
   - Stepper sequence with G9 bilingual labels (`Scheduled` -> `On the way` -> `Arrived` -> `Work in progress` -> `Completed`).
   - Current status marked with accessible text tokens (A11Y-003).
   - Customer address snapshot, scheduled time, and crew size display.
   - Direct Call and Message customer action triggers (`CHAT-002`).
   - Unpaid commission block notice preventing start if commission is owed (`D-021`, `LEDGER-NEG-005`).
   - Awaiting approval notice when pending extras exist (`BOOK-STATUS-010`).
   - Safety problem stop modal (`BOOK-CANCEL-006`) and Customer not here modal (`BOOK-CANCEL-005`).
   - 5 canonical screen states (`loading`, `empty`, `success`, `error`, `pending`).

4. **P13 Extras & Materials Propose-Only Screen (`BP §13 P13`, `BP §58.1`, `§58.2`, `FARE-003..012`):**
   - Invariant enforced: Provider can **ONLY PROPOSE** extra charges; proposed lines are strictly non-payable until customer approval (`FARE-012`, `PRICE-007`).
   - 7 provider line types supported (`Labour`, `Parts`, `Tools`, `Consumables`, `Emergency`, `Travel`, `Discount`).
   - Platform fee strictly absent (`D-025`).
   - Tools reason required (`FARE-004`).
   - Materials classification: `Included`, `Not included`, `Extra (needs approval)` (`PRICE-005`).
   - Crew size change proposal without altering base price without approved lines (`PROV-CREW-004`, `D-018`).
   - 5 canonical screen states.

5. **P14 Completion Evidence & Billing Screen (`BP §13 P14`, `UX-PROV-005`, `BOOK-STATUS-009`, `D-003`, `D-006`, `D-016`, `D-030`):**
   - Two-step sequential workflow:
     - Step 1: Work notes, photos picker with before/after thumbnails, customer signature, completion code validation, and "Mark job complete".
     - Step 2: Rendered **strictly after** the job is marked `Completed` (`D-003`).
   - Final bill breakdown: Customer total, Labour portion, Materials portion, Tax (Rs. 0 while unset, `D-029`).
   - 30% commission calculated strictly on labour lines only (`D-006`, `D-015`, `COMM-004`).
   - Provider share = Customer Total - Commission.
   - "How was it paid?":
     - `CASH` choice: Cash confirm sheet displays exact commission debited from balance (`D-006`, `COMM-001`).
     - `DIGITAL` choice: Recorded as `WAITING`, gateway-confirmed only (`ARCH §6.8`, `ABUSE-007`).
   - 5 canonical screen states.

6. **Design & Accessibility:**
