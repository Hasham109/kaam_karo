## 1. What was done

1. **Provider Earnings Ledger (`LedgerService`):**
   - Implemented in `services/api/src/modules/payments/ledger.service.ts`.
   - Records double-entry provider ledger transactions (`provider_ledger_entry` table) per ARCH §6.8 & BP §26.
   - **Cash Settlement:** Customer pays provider directly in cash. Ledger debits 30% of labour (`COMMISSION_DEBIT`, D-016, COMM-003) as liability owed to the platform.
   - **Digital Settlement:** Customer pays platform via gateway. Ledger credits total bill − platform commission (`JOB_CREDIT`, D-016) as net earnings payable to the provider.
   - **Worked Example Verified:** Labour Rs. 2,000 (200,000 paisa) + Parts Rs. 1,500 (150,000 paisa) + Visit Rs. 300 (30,000 paisa) = Rs. 3,800. Cash job debits Rs. 600; Digital job credits Rs. 3,200 (ARCH §6.8).
   - **Balance Accounting:** Real-time computation `Balance = SUM(credits) − SUM(debits)`. Negative balances are allowed and tracked as unpaid commission liabilities (D-016, D-020, LEDGER-004).
   - **Single Settlement Guard:** Strictly prevents duplicate ledger entries per booking (`LEDGER-002`, `REL-008`).

2. **Atomic DB Transactions:**
   - Wrapped `recordCashPayment` and `confirmDigitalPayment` in atomic `prisma.$transaction`.
   - Payment record creation, payment method record, and provider ledger entry write occur atomically in one database transaction (D-016, REL-008).

3. **Strict RBAC & Access Control Enforcement:**
   - In `services/api/src/modules/payments/payments.controller.ts`:
     - Secured all endpoints with `AuthGuard`. Removed `@Public()` from all endpoints except gateway callback `confirmPayment`.
     - Provider endpoints (`/v1/payments/provider/:id/balance`, `/v1/payments/provider/:id/ledger`) require `UserRoleType.PROVIDER` and enforce ownership via `assertProviderOwnsProfile` (BP §29).
     - Admin ledger endpoint requires `UserRoleType.ADMIN` + `AdminSubRole.FINANCE` or `SUPER_ADMIN` + verified MFA.
     - Cash payment recording restricted to the assigned provider of the completed booking (`D-030`).
   - Regression test suite added: `services/api/src/modules/payments/__tests__/payments-access.spec.ts` (7 tests).

4. **UI Billing & Execution Integration:**
   - **P14 Completion & Billing (`P14CompletionEvidenceScreen.tsx`):**
     - Two-step flow: completion evidence code validation followed by final billing.
     - "How was it paid?" CASH / DIGITAL choice with commission split note (D-006, D-016, D-030).
     - Cash confirm sheet shows commission debit notice; digital status shows gateway confirmation status.
   - **C20 Customer Execution Tracking (`C20CompletionConfirmScreen.tsx`):**
     - Displays "Paid by: Cash to Provider" or "Paid by: Digital (Confirmed)" badge.
     - KK-T5 anti-fraud protection ("Paid by is wrong" / "I paid cash" report action, OQ-077).

5. **OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract:**
   - `contracts/payments.yaml` includes `/v1/payments/provider/{providerProfileId}/balance`, `/v1/payments/provider/{providerProfileId}/ledger`, and `/v1/payments/cash-record`.
