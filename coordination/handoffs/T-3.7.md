1. **OpenAPI 3.1.0 Contract (`contracts/matching.yaml` v0.1.0):**

   - Operations: `findMatchesForRequest`, `getMatchCandidates`, `getMatchingConfig`, `updateMatchingConfig`.
2. **Hard Eligibility Filters 73–79 & Unpaid Commission (`BP §54.1`, `D-020`):**
   - Evaluates provider standing, skill mapping, service area coverage (radius / zone), live slot availability, minimum verification level, active safety disputes, and job capacity limit.

3. **Multi-Signal Scoring & Dynamic Weights (`MATCH-SCORE-001..013`, `D-010`):**
   - 13 normalized signals [0.0, 1.0] with Bayesian smoothed rating volume and minimum job sample size for repeat customer rate.
   - Dynamic weight updates without deployment (`OPS-ADMIN-021`), stamping `ranking_version` on every run.
4. **Algorithmic Fairness & Supplier Protection (`MATCH-FAIR-001..003`):**
   - New provider boost within 30-day window; market concentration caps.
5. **Human-Readable Explanations (`MATCH-EXPL-001`):**
   - Bilingual "Why am I seeing this?" reasons in Urdu and English.
6. **Supply Gap Recovery (`MATCH-GAP-001`, `BP §61.3`):**
   - Transitions to `NO_SUPPLY` on 0 eligible matches and provides structured recovery options (broaden radius, reschedule, priority callback, waitlist, related service).
7. **Zero Auto-Assignment Guarantee (`D-005`, `MATCH-MODE-001`):**
   - Strictly discovery-only; customer chooses provider. Never creates or modifies bookings.
