## 1. Scope & Accomplishments

Delivered partner incoming job requests, job details, accept/decline action sheet, and request media player covering **P08–P10, and P25** (`BP §52.2`, `BP §62.2`, `BP §85`, `BP §76.1`, `BP §13 P08..P10, P25`; `PROF-ADDR-004`, `PROV-CREW-001..006`, `D-004`, `D-018`, `MEDIA-005..006`):

1. **Pre-Match Privacy Guard (`BP §52.2`, `§62.2`, `§85`, `PROF-ADDR-004`):**
   - Strictly enforces that pre-match job requests and details expose zero customer PII (phone number, customer full name, street address, and house/plot number are `undefined`).
   - Only neighbourhood area name and distance are displayed pre-match (e.g. "Gulberg III · 3.2 km away").
   - Explicit informational notice: *"Exact address and contact are shared after matching."*

2. **P08 Incoming Job Requests Screen (`BP §13 P08`, `BP §85`, `BP §62.2`, `UX-PROV-001..003`, `QUOTE-002`, `PROF-ADDR-004`):**
   - Implemented in `apps/partner/src/screens/P08JobRequestsScreen.tsx`.
   - Incoming job cards displaying service name, summary, area name and distance away.
   - Commercial model tags: `Instant Booking`, `Customer Chose You`, `Quote Requested`.
   - Commercial pricing display: `Fixed Price`, `From`, `Inspection Quote / Visit fee` via `formatPkr`.
   - Media badges linking directly to P25 media player.
   - 2-tap response flow (Accept / Send Quote / Ask / Decline).
   - Supports 5 canonical screen states (`loading`, `empty`, `success`, `error`, `pending`).

3. **P09 Job Detail Screen (`BP §13 P09`, `BP §85`, `PROF-ADDR-004`, `PROV-CREW-002`, `PROV-CREW-006`):**
   - Implemented in `apps/partner/src/screens/P09JobDetailScreen.tsx`.
   - Comprehensive job specifications and status banner.
   - Pre-match location notice.
   - Post-match reveal: full street address and direct call/message actions only when `status === 'ACCEPTED'`.
   - Crew size and verified team leader display.
   - Cancel job action with confirmation dialog warning of reliability impacts.
   - Supports 5 canonical screen states.

4. **P10 Accept / Decline Action Sheet (`BP §13 P10`, `BP §62.2`, `BP §85`, `D-004`, `D-018`, `PROV-CREW-001..002`, `PROV-CREW-005`):**
   - Implemented in `apps/partner/src/screens/P10ActionSheet.tsx`.
   - Bottom sheet modal for rapid decision-making.
   - Crew size stepper (≥ 1 worker).
   - Prominent notice enforcing `D-018` / `PROV-CREW-005`: *"The number of workers does not change the price."* (`کاریگروں کی تعداد سے قیمت نہیں بدلتی۔`).
   - 2-tap Confirm Accept / Confirm Decline actions.

5. **P25 Request Media Player (`BP §76.1`, `ARCH §3.2`, `MEDIA-005`, `MEDIA-006`):**
   - Implemented in `apps/partner/src/screens/P25MediaPlayerScreen.tsx`.

   - Video player with thumbnail preview and play/pause controls.
   - Photo gallery pager with "Photo {index} of {total}" indicators and next/prev controls.
   - In-flow close action returning directly to the job request.
   - Supports 5 canonical screen states.

6. **Accessibility & Device Matrix (`docs/qa/device-matrix.md`):**
