## 1. Summary of Completed Work

1. **Contracts Specification (`contracts/availability.yaml`):**
   - Published REST endpoints and data models for low-rating enforcement and partner standing:
     - `GET /v1/providers/{providerId}/rating-standing`: Partner inspection endpoint returning standing details, warning count, current rating, threshold, localized notice copy (en/ur), and matching eligibility.
     - `POST /v1/admin/trust/rating-enforcement/run`: Operations / Risk & Trust manual job trigger with optional `dryRun` override and detailed per-provider report.
     - `GET /v1/admin/trust/standing/{providerProfileId}`: Admin audit inspection of provider standing.
     - `POST /v1/admin/trust/standing/{providerProfileId}/reinstate`: Admin reinstatement endpoint requiring mandatory audited reason (`≥ 5` characters) and `AdminSubRole` of `OPERATIONS` or `RISK_TRUST`.
   - Contract test suite validated (`@kaamkaro/contracts`: 53/53 passed).

2. **Backend Domain Logic (`services/api/src/modules/trust/`):**
   - `rating-enforcement.types.ts`: Defined data structures, interfaces, and DTOs (`ProviderEnforcementEvaluation`, `RatingEnforcementReportDto`, `RatingStandingDetailsDto`, `ReinstateProviderDto`, `RunEnforcementJobOptions`).
   - `rating-enforcement.service.ts`:
     - **RATING-ENF-001**: Scheduled job evaluating provider average public rating over configured window.
     - **RATING-ENF-002**: Only `VISIBLE` reviews are counted; moderated-out (`REMOVED`) or flagged unreviewed reviews are excluded from calculations.
     - **RATING-ENF-003 / D-017**: Strictly triggers when average rating is `< 2.0` (e.g. 1.99 triggers; exactly 2.0 remains in Good Standing).
     - **RATING-ENF-004 / D-014**: Progressive warning sequence (Warning 1 → Warning 2) respecting configurable grace period (`rating.review_period_days`). Full recovery restores `GOOD_STANDING` and resets warning count when average rating rises back to `≥ 2.0`.
     - **RATING-ENF-005**: Provider account blocked (`BLOCKED_LOW_RATING`) with reason code `LOW_RATING` upon exceeding configured warning threshold (`rating.warnings_before_block`).
     - **RATING-ENF-006 / ROLE-003**: Admin reinstatement by Operations or Risk/Trust staff with audited reason (`≥ 5` chars) recorded via `AuditWriter`.
     - **RATING-ENF-007 / NOTIF-008**: Dispatches outbox domain events (`provider.rating_warning`, `provider.blocked_low_rating`, `provider.reinstated`) via transactional `OutboxWriter` with `schemaVersion: 1`.
     - **RATING-ENF-008 / D-017 / OQ-013**: Default dry-run mode when either `rating.min_reviews_for_enforcement` or `rating.review_period_days` is unconfigured (`null`), safely evaluating and reporting without altering database standing or dispatching blocking actions.
     - **MATCH-ELIG-073**: Provider matching exclusion enforced when standing is `BLOCKED_LOW_RATING`.
   - `rating-enforcement.controller.ts`: Implemented `ProviderRatingStandingController` and `AdminRatingEnforcementController` protected by JWT auth and `RequireAdminSubRoleGuard`.
   - Unit test suite: `services/api/src/modules/trust/__tests__/rating-enforcement.service.spec.ts` (16/16 tests passing).

3. **Phase 7 Integration & Harmonization:**
   - Exported rating enforcement service and controllers in `services/api/src/modules/trust/index.ts` and registered in `TrustModule`.
   - Admin UI components (`A11ReviewsModerationScreen`, `A12SupportScreen`) aligned with strict typing and standard screen states.
