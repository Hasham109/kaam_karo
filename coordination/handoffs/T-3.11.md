## 1. Scope & Accomplishments

Delivered Voice Assistant v1 for the Customer App (IMPL §6 T3.11; BP §77, BP §78; VOICE-001..008; PERM-003, PERM-JUST-002; A11Y-002, A11Y-004; I18N-001..007):

1. **Voice Assistant Screen & UI (`apps/customer/src/screens/C38VoiceAssistantScreen.tsx`):**
   - **Push-to-Talk Button (`c38-ptt-button`):** Explicit customer tap initiates audio capture. Returns to idle after utterance; strictly zero background listening or wake words (`VOICE-001`).
   - **Listening Indicator (`c38-listening-indicator`):** Visible animated badge and status indicator rendered while the microphone is active (`VOICE-002`).
   - **Recognized Speech Preview (`c38-heard-card`, `c38-heard-text`):** Displays transcribed text prior to applying consequential actions or navigation (`VOICE-003`).
   - **Undo Trigger (`c38-undo-button`):** Direct tap and voice trigger to revert recent draft modifications (`VOICE-004`).
   - **Text Fallback Bar (`c38-text-input`, `c38-apply-button`):** Full operational parity for text-based input when voice is unavailable or declined (`VOICE-005`).
   - **Language Toggle Chip (`c38-language-chip`):** In-screen language toggle between English and Urdu (`VOICE-006`).
   - **Consequential Action Confirmation Modal (`c38-confirm-modal`):** Mandatory blocking modal dialog with target action details, confirm button (`c38-modal-confirm-button`), and cancel button (`c38-modal-cancel-button`) before performing `PLACE_BOOKING`, `ACCEPT_QUOTE`, `PAY_BOOKING`, `CANCEL_BOOKING`, or `SUBMIT_COMPLAINT` (`VOICE-007`).
   - **Educational Permission Pre-Prompt:** `PermissionModal` with `kind="microphone"` explaining audio requirements before triggering device OS prompt (`PERM-003`, `PERM-JUST-002`).

2. **Voice Assistant Engine & Intent Parser (`apps/customer/src/services/voice-assistant.ts`):**
   - **Trilingual Intent Engine (`VOICE-006`):** Supports English, Urdu script (Nastaliq/Arabic), and Roman Urdu across navigation, booking draft mutations, search, quoting, cancellation, and support.
   - **Strict Allow-List Enforcement (`VOICE-008`, `BP §77.3`):** Out-of-bounds keywords (account deletion, fund transfers, banking, personal contacts) are checked first and rejected with standard unsupported copy.
   - **Multi-Level Undo Stack (`VOICE-004`):** Maintains history of request drafts, rolling back smoothly on "undo", "cancel that", "wapas karo", or "pehle jaisa karo".
   - **Word-Boundary Matching:** Robust regex matching for technical terms preventing false positives on substring occurrences.

3. **Routing & Navigation Integration:**
   - Registered `C38` route in `apps/customer/src/App.tsx`.
   - Wired Home Screen (`C06`) voice intake button (`c06-voice-button`) to navigate to `C38` with return target.

4. **Design System, i18n & Accessibility Compliance:**
   - 100% localized message catalogs in English and Urdu (`packages/i18n/src/messages/customer.ts`).
   - 0 hardcoded user-facing strings; `guard:string-lint` verified across 39 UI files.
   - RTL mirroring and Noto Nastaliq Urdu typography with 2.5× line height in Urdu (`BP §78`, `I18N-001..004`).
   - 48×48 dp minimum touch targets across all touchables (`minTapTargetStyle()`; `A11Y-002`).
